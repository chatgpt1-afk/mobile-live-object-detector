First test

  1. Print the black-and-white 5 cm marker at 100% / Actual Size.
  2. Tap Open Camera, point at the empty background and choose the final phone position.
  3. While the scene is empty, tap Set Empty Background and keep the phone still.
  4. Place the item and one marker in the same plane, then tap Detect Object.
  5. Make sure the green frame contains the complete item. Keep the marker visible elsewhere inside the camera view.
  6. If the live item frame does not appear, Capture is still available and the photo will be analysed after shooting.
  7. Tap the round Camera icon.
  8. The captured photo is then analysed for the marker and item separately.
