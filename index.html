First test

  1. Print `reference/5cm_reference_marker.pdf` at 100% / Actual Size.
  2. Use a plain background with even light.
  3. Tap Start Camera and allow camera access.
  4. Set the final phone position and distance, then remove the item.
  5. Tap Calibrate Empty Background and keep the phone still.
  6. Place the item and 5 cm marker in the same plane, with a clear gap between them.
  7. Wait for the green object frame and cyan marker frame.
  8. Tap Capture to analyse and crop the high-resolution photo.
  9. Recalibrate only if the phone position, zoom, lighting or background changes.
